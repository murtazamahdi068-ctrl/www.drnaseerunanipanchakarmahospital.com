About the Hospital

Where Traditional Wisdom Meets Modern Healthcare.

Dr. Naseer’s Unani & Panchkarma Hospital is a healthcare facility on Eidgah Road, Srinagar, offering Unani and Panchkarma-based care alongside supporting therapies, laboratory services and diagnostics.

Publicly listed services include Unani medicine, Panchkarma, skin & laser treatments, physiotherapy, leech therapy, cupping therapy and laboratory & diagnostic support — brought together under one roof for patients across Kashmir.
